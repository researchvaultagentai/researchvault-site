Open your private case

  1. Keep the invention private

    Verify your email and acknowledge the signed confidentiality undertaking. Then upload your invention materials in the private case: Word or PDF drafts, drawings, images, test results and MP4 video up to 20 MiB and 7 minutes.

  2. Review the prior-art report

    Stage 1 costs US$1,111 under the separate order: US$111 for review and consultation, US$555 to start the specialist report after consultation, and US$445 to unlock the report’s opening password and Word copy. We make the locked PDF available when the report is ready.

  3. Choose an invention and sign

    Describe your chosen invention in writing. If the team accepts the PCT engagement, review the separate case quote and sign in the portal using an email code. You can also download and return a signed copy. The quote shows your exact payments and keeps official fees separate.

  4. Review Word drafts

    After the first PCT payment and completion of your filing details, the team starts your draft. Download Word v1.0 when it appears. Use Word comments or Track Changes and return the annotated DOCX to the same version. Review each new version until ready. Use Request an online meeting if you want to discuss changes.

    Approve the current draft only when you want us to prepare the final package. This draft approval does not authorize ePCT submission.

  5. Settle and authorize the final package

    When the final package is ready, pay the filing instalment. After confirmation, open the approval copy and read the final authorization. Tick the acknowledgement and confirm with a new email code. This locks the exact package for ePCT submission and unlocks the final files and your signed authorization record.

  6. Get the official filing receipt

    Keep control of your WIPO account. Grant case access through official ePCT permissions, with our guidance if needed. Once the authorized team starts submission, the portal shows the status. After official confirmation, download the ePCT receipt and filed documents from the same case.

  7. Follow the ISA and completion

    Our preparation typically takes 7–25 days from complete prerequisites. An agreed response or amendment targets up to 7 days from the required official materials and instructions. Time awaiting the ISA or another office is separate. The conditional completion fee is due after agreed work is complete and the official notice confirms the scheduled publication date. National or regional phase entry is outside the base engagement.

  8. Read your case status

    The portal shows whose turn it is: yours, our team’s, or an official authority’s. Open team actions have a 72-hour target for a reply or progress update. If that target passes, the case shows Update overdue. The case shows a separate status while the authority processes it. A private message or other open team action can still have its own timer. Official deadlines and delivery timelines are shown separately when available. New team updates, shared files and major case milestones appear in the case notifications.
