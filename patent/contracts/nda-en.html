INVENTION CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (NDA)

Version: NDA-EN-v1

This agreement protects confidential technical and commercial information disclosed for patentability analysis, invention development, drafting, filing, and related agreed services.

1. Confidential Information

Confidential information includes invention descriptions, drawings, engineering files, algorithms, test data, prototypes, business information, and other non-public materials disclosed for the engagement.

2. Permitted Use

Confidential information will be used only for the agreed patent-related engagement and authorized filing activities.

3. Non-Disclosure

Confidential information will not be disclosed without authorization except where reasonably necessary for authorized professional services, official filing procedures, or legal requirements.

4. Exclusions

Information already public, independently developed, lawfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law is excluded to the applicable extent.

5. Survival

Confidentiality obligations survive completion or discontinuation of the engagement while the information remains confidential.

6. Electronic Acceptance

OTP verification, typed name, drawn signature, server timestamp, document hash and evidence HMAC may be retained as technical evidence of acceptance.
