PATENT CONSULTANT / PCT INTERNATIONAL-PHASE SERVICES AGREEMENT

Version: PCT-EN-v1

This agreement governs the project-specific professional services selected for the Client's PCT matter. The project quotation and Fee Schedule control the exact scope and pricing.

1. Scope

Services may include patentability analysis, prior-art analysis, invention development, patent drafting, claims, standard patent drawings, PCT preparation and filing, and agreed procedural handling.

2. Stage 1

The Patentability, Prior-Art & Invention Development stage is an independent professional deliverable. Its fee becomes non-refundable once substantive professional work begins.

3. Engineering Development

Electronic design, Altium work, CAD, control architecture, algorithm development, simulation, prototype or experimental validation are separate engineering services unless expressly included in the written scope.

4. Fees

Professional fees are defined by project-specific invoices and milestones. Official PCT fees and third-party costs are separate unless expressly included.

5. National Phase

National-phase entry, national prosecution, local foreign counsel, national translations, national patent-office fees, grant fees and annuities are excluded unless separately agreed in writing.

6. Contractual Completion

For the base PCT engagement, services are deemed completed upon issuance and availability in WIPO/ePCT of the official communication confirming the scheduled international publication date. The Consultant is not required to remain engaged until the actual publication date.

7. ISR / Written Opinion

Detailed post-ISR strategy, Article 19 amendments, Chapter II / Article 34 procedures and substantive written arguments are separate services unless expressly included.

8. Client Responsibilities

The Client must provide complete and accurate technical information, prior disclosures, prior filings and inventor information, and must review drafts within the agreed period.

9. Confidentiality

Confidential invention information is used only for the engagement and authorized filing activities, subject to lawful disclosure requirements.

10. No Guarantee

No patent grant, favorable search opinion, commercial success, licensing result or freedom-to-operate outcome is guaranteed.

11. Deliverables and Ownership

Upon full payment of the applicable stage, transferable economic rights in Client-specific final deliverables are transferred to the Client to the extent legally transferable. Pre-existing methodologies, templates, workflows and general know-how remain with the Consultant. Inventorship must follow actual inventive contribution.

12. Electronic Acceptance

OTP verification, typed name, drawn signature, server timestamp, document hash and evidence HMAC may be retained as technical evidence of electronic acceptance.
